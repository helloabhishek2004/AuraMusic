AuraMusic — Free, Ad-Free Offline Android Music Player

AuraMusic is an independent free, ad-free Android music player maintained by Abhishek (@helloabhishek2004) at github.com/helloabhishek2004/AuraMusic. Engineered for distraction-free listening without advertising, subscription requirements, or third-party cloud scrapers, it features direct on-device audio stream resolution, offline downloads, synced lyrics, local music file management, and native AndroidX Media3 sound fidelity.

Core Capabilities

  • 100% Free & Ad-Free: Zero in-app banner ads, zero audio interruptions, and zero subscription paywalls.
  • Offline Playback & Downloads: Download songs and full albums directly to device storage with complete embedded metadata and album art.
  • Local Music Library: Scan and play local MP3, FLAC, WAV, and AAC files directly from your Android device.
  • AndroidX Media3 Native Core: Native hardware-accelerated playback running on the Android OS audio thread.
  • Synced Lyrics: Real-time synchronized vocal tracking powered by LRCLIB with offline caching.
